Platinum: Vice Got a Price! — 0 of 54 others earned

Roadmap to the platinum

Play it in five stages, in order. Story first — the chapter and mission trophies come free as you play — then the stranger strands, then collectibles and exploration, then activities and combat, and finally the money grinds. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops with the last trophy you earn.

    Counts come straight from the official descriptions: 42 Shooting Range challenges, 250 stunts, 100 WAiNK It locations, 100 vehicles, 52 areas of Leonida, 18 meditation spots, 14 drone-race locations, 20 races, 8 stranger strands, 5 bank vaults, 9 gangs, 8 garages, 3 card sets, 50 challenges, 500 choices, $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, 10 Scooter Bros registrations.

    Missable trophies — do these on time

    Twelve trophies are tied to a specific moment, a partner being with you, or a one-shot setup. They are flagged MISSABLE in the checklist too. The rest can all be finished in free roam after the story.
